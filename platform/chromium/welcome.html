Welcome to Boring YouTube

You've just unlocked distraction-free viewing. Here's how to get the most out of it.

How it works

1

Click the extension icon 📺

Find the Boring YouTube icon in your Chrome toolbar, next to the address bar.

2

Toggle Boring Mode ON 🌙

Flip the sun/moon switch to activate greyscale thumbnails across all of YouTube.

3

Browse with intention ✅

Without colourful clickbait, you'll only click on videos you actually want to watch.

💡

Pro tip: Pin the extension to your toolbar for instant access. Right-click the 🧩 puzzle icon → "Pin Boring YouTube".
